Welcome

A quiet compound off the Rumuolumeni road

Open in maps

Top 2 sits just back from the road in Mgboudohia, behind the charcoal facade you will recognise from the sign. Inside the gate there is parking, a lounge, a restaurant, a meeting room, a shawarma hut at the gate and a hall that seats a wedding — and rooftop solar with battery backup, so a power cut does not reach your air conditioning.

Easy to find

1 Charles Ogun Street, Mgboudohia, Rumuolumeni — west Port Harcourt, off the Rumuolumeni road.

Open in maps

Power that holds

Rooftop solar feeding a battery bank, with a generator behind it. Rooms and the hall stay lit and cool through the usual outages.

Someone is always at the desk

Reception is staffed around the clock, behind a manned gate with CCTV through the corridors and free parking inside the wall.
